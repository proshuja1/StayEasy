Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • 48 hours or more before check-in: 100% refund
  • 24 to 48 hours before check-in: 50% refund
  • Less than 24 hours or no-show: no refund

Refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Booking Changes

Dates, guests and rooms can be changed before check-in. The updated total is shown before you confirm. Any excess amount paid is refunded.

Payments

Pay in full, or secure your room with a 25% advance and pay the rest at the hotel. UPI, cards and net banking are supported. Prices include 12% GST.
